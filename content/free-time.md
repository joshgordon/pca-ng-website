### 1. Patapsco Valley State Park - McKeldin Area

- **Distance**: 4 miles (10-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "11676 Marriottsville Rd, Marriottsville, MD 21104" >}}
- **Description**: The McKeldin area of Patapsco Valley State Park offers scenic hiking
  trails, river views, and opportunities for picnicking, horseback riding, and disc golf.
- **Cost**: $3 per vehicle (Maryland residents), $5 per vehicle (non-residents)

### 2. Historic Ellicott City

- **Distance**: 8 miles (15-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "Main Street, Ellicott City, MD 21043" >}}
- **Description**: A charming historic town with unique shops, restaurants, and historic
  sites like the B&O Railroad Museum and Ellicott Mills. Perfect for strolling and exploring.
- **Cost**: Free to explore

### 3. Historic Sykesville

- **Distance**: 6 miles (10-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "Main Street, Sykesville, MD 21784" >}}
- **Description**: A quaint historic town with unique shops, restaurants, and historic sites.
  Voted ‘Best Small Town’ in America by Budget Travel Magazine. Perfect for strolling
  and exploring.
- **Cost**: Free

### 4. Centennial Park

- **Distance**: 10 miles (18-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "10000 Clarksville Pike, Ellicott City, MD 21042" >}}
- **Description**: A large park featuring a scenic lake with a 2.4-mile paved trail, perfect for
  walking, jogging, or enjoying the serene water views.
- **Cost**: Free

### 5. Clark's Elioak Farm

- **Distance**: 10 miles (15-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "10500 Clarksville Pike, Ellicott City, MD 21042" >}}
- **Description**: A family-friendly farm with a petting zoo, hayrides, and a seasonal
  pumpkin patch. Fun for families or anyone who enjoys farm life.
- **Hours**: 10 AM – 5 PM
- **Cost**: $8 per person

### 6. The Shrine of St. Anthony

- **Distance**: 6 miles (10-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "12290 Folly Quarter Rd, Ellicott City, MD 21042" >}}
- **Description**: A peaceful Franciscan monastery on 310 acres of rolling countryside. The
  grounds are open for quiet reflection and walking.
- **Hours**: 9 AM – 5 PM
- **Cost**: Free (donations welcome)

### 7. Guinness Open Gate Brewery

- **Distance**: 17 miles (25-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "5001 Washington Blvd, Halethorpe, MD 21227" >}}
- **Description**: The only Guinness brewery in North America, offering tours, tastings, and
  a restaurant with locally-inspired dishes. Great for beer enthusiasts.
- **Hours**: 11 AM – 9 PM
- **Cost**: Free to enter; tasting flights and tours available at additional cost

### 8. Fells Point (Baltimore)

- **Distance**: 25 miles (40-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "Thames Street, Baltimore, MD 21231" >}}
- **Description**: A historic waterfront neighborhood with cobblestone streets, historic
  pubs, eclectic shops, and waterfront views. Perfect for a relaxing stroll along the harbor.
- **Cost**: Free to explore

### 9. Sagamore Spirit Distillery

- **Distance**: 22 miles (30-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "301 E Cromwell St, Baltimore, MD 21230" >}}
- **Description**: A whiskey distillery offering guided tours and tastings, featuring award-winning
  rye whiskey and insights into the distillation process.
- **Hours**: 12 PM – 6 PM
- **Cost**: $22 per person for the tour

### 10. The Walters Art Museum

- **Distance**: 22 miles (30-minute drive)
- **Address**: {{< addr "600 N Charles St, Baltimore, MD 21201" >}}
- **Description**: A renowned art museum with a vast collection of pieces ranging from
  ancient art to 19th-century European masterpieces.
- **Hours**: 10 AM – 5 PM (Wednesday – Sunday)
- **Cost**: Free admission
